PlainCup Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

In short: PlainCup has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to the internet for its own purposes. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What PlainCup stores, and where

  • Your drink log (which drinks you logged, the time, the amount, saved custom drinks): stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Your settings and profile (bedtime and wake-up time, and the optional details that make the estimate more accurate: body weight, smoking, oral contraceptives, pregnancy, certain medicines, a half-life you know, how sensitive you are to caffeine): stored only on your device. All of these are optional and are used only to compute the estimate shown to you.
  • Widgets and the Live Activity: the app, its widgets and the Lock Screen Live Activity share your data through an App Group on your own device so they can show the same number. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Background refresh: iOS may briefly wake PlainCup so the widget and Live Activity stay current. This work happens entirely on your device.

Purchases

PlainCup can be bought as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or your name. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Whether a plan is active is kept on your device so the app and its widgets can unlock.

What PlainCup does not do

  • It has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email.
  • It does not read Apple Health, your location, contacts, photos or camera.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

Not medical advice

PlainCup gives estimates for general wellness, based on published research. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything. The numbers are approximate and vary between people. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medicines, talk to a health professional about caffeine. PlainCup is intended for adults (18+).

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the app removes it. Your device's own backups (iCloud or computer backups you have turned on in iOS) may include the app's data, under Apple's terms and your own settings.

Children

PlainCup is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
