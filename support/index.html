Common questions

Do I need an account? No. PlainCup has no accounts. Everything stays on your device.

How do I log a drink? On the Today screen tap Add drink, swipe the wheel, then double-tap the drink in the middle. For anything not on the wheel, choose More to see the whole list, set a custom amount, or log a drink you had earlier.

What does "Can I drink that?" do? Before you log, it shows what a drink would do to your caffeine level at bedtime, when you would be sleep-ready, and how much room you have left today.

Are the numbers exact? No. They are estimates from a standard model of how the body clears caffeine, adjusted by what you tell the app. Drink strengths are typical values and vary a lot. See "How the model works" in Settings.

The Lock Screen Live Activity doesn't show. In iOS Settings > PlainCup, make sure Live Activities are allowed, then log a drink or start it from the app.

The widget shows an old number. Widgets refresh on a schedule set by iOS. Open the app once and it updates.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. All of your data is stored on your device only.

Is PlainCup medical advice? No. It gives general wellness estimates. Talk to a health professional if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medicines.
